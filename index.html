Most summarisers give you three buttons and one opinion. Lens gives you a dial. Turn it down and the document collapses to its thesis; turn it up and the supporting detail comes back into focus — recomputed live, with every sentence traceable to the page it came from.

  • PDF text extracted with layout & heading reconstruction
  • Images & scans read with OCR, after automatic contrast repair
  • No key, no server — the ranking engine is written from scratch
